Examination

HN College of Management, being an Autonomous College, designs and conducts its own examinations as per the Autonomous Academic Council guidelines aligned with NEP 2020.

📝

Internal Assessment (IA)

Conducted twice per semester through unit tests, assignments, presentations, and surprise tests. Carries 20% weightage of total marks.

📄

Mid Semester Examination

Held mid-semester covering syllabus up to the point of examination. Carries 20% weightage. Open-book style permitted for certain subjects.

🎓

End Semester Examination (ESE)

Comprehensive examination conducted at the end of each semester covering the full syllabus. Carries 60% weightage of total marks.

📊

Practical & Viva

Practicals, project work, and viva voce form part of the internal/external evaluation for applicable subjects and programmes.

Grading System (10-Point Scale)

Grade Grade Point Percentage Range Classification

D (Pass) 4 40% – 44.99% Pass
F (Fail) 0 Below 40% Fail

Examination Rules

  • Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory to appear for the End Semester Examination.
  • Students must carry their college ID card and examination hall ticket to the examination hall.
  • Use of unfair means (copying, electronic devices) will result in cancellation of the paper and disciplinary action.
  • Re-examination / KT (Keep Term) facilities are available as per university ordinance for failed candidates.
  • Answer scripts may be applied for re-verification / re-evaluation within the prescribed time after result declaration.

Result

Results for all programmes are declared within 30 days of the End Semester Examination. Students can view their results using the links below.

HN College of Management follows a transparent and timely result publication process. Semester results are declared on the college notice board and are also available for viewing online. Mark sheets are distributed after verification by the Examination Department.

In case of queries regarding results, students can contact the Examination Cell during office hours. Re-verification and re-evaluation forms are available at the office counter within 15 days of result declaration.

Library Services & Facilities

The college library offers a wide range of services to support the academic and research needs of students and faculty. The library operates six days a week from 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM.

  • Book lending facility — up to 3 books per student at a time
  • Reference and reading room with seating for 80+ students
  • Internet and Wi-Fi access for digital learning and research
  • Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC) for book search
  • E-library access — NPTEL, SWAYAM, National Digital Library
  • Photocopying and printing facility within premises
  • Current Awareness Service (CAS) — notifications on new arrivals
  • Document Delivery Service for interlibrary resources
  • Lending of previous year question paper banks
  • Newspaper reading section — leading national and regional dailies
  • Research support for project work, dissertations, and seminars
  • Display of new books and journal articles of interest
