Institute Information

Hirachand Nemchand College of Management, Solapur is an Autonomous College affiliated to P.A.H. Solapur University, NAAC Re-accredited with an 'A' Grade — a premier institution shaping management professionals since 1994.

The college is run under the aegis of the Shri Hirachand Nemchand Mafatal Punamchand Educational Trust, Solapur, a pioneering educational trust committed to providing quality professional education to students of the Solapur region and beyond.

As an Autonomous Institution, the college designs its own syllabi, conducts its own examinations, and adopts innovative pedagogical approaches aligned with contemporary industry demands. This ensures students receive the most relevant and forward-looking management education.

HN College of Management offers postgraduate MBA and undergraduate BBA and BCA programmes. The college is recognised for its rigorous academic environment, experienced faculty, industry connections, and a strong placement cell that consistently delivers excellent outcomes for students.

Vision and Mission

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in management education, research, and entrepreneurship — shaping socially responsible, globally competent leaders who drive sustainable and inclusive growth.

Our Mission

  • To provide high-quality, value-based management education that develops professional competence, ethical grounding, and social responsibility.
  • To foster a research and innovation culture that encourages critical thinking and intellectual enquiry among students and faculty.
  • To build strong industry-academia partnerships that create real-world learning and meaningful career opportunities for students.
  • To promote inclusive education ensuring accessibility to students from all socio-economic backgrounds through scholarships and support mechanisms.
  • To contribute to regional and national development through community engagement, social entrepreneurship, and outreach programmes.
  • To continuously upgrade curriculum, infrastructure, and pedagogy in alignment with national education policy and global management standards.

Goals and Quality Policy

HN College of Management is committed to continuous improvement in academic quality, student outcomes, and institutional effectiveness through a structured IQAC-driven quality assurance framework.

Institutional Goals

  • Achieve 100% programme outcomes through effective teaching-learning and robust evaluation processes.
  • Enhance faculty development through regular FDPs, research publications, and collaborative industry projects.
  • Strengthen the Training & Placement Cell to ensure maximum student employability in reputed organisations at competitive packages.
  • Develop entrepreneurial mindset through incubation support, mentorship, and start-up awareness and funding programmes.
  • Sustain a healthy student-to-faculty ratio with personalised academic mentoring to minimise dropouts and maximise achievement.
  • Foster digital literacy and embed technology in all academic and administrative functions for efficiency and transparency.
  • Maintain green campus initiatives and environmental accountability as core institutional values.

Quality Policy

The institution follows the IQAC-driven quality enhancement cycle — Plan, Do, Check, Act (PDCA) — to systematically review all academic and administrative activities. Annual Academic Audit Reports and NAAC criteria compliance reports are prepared and reviewed at the institutional level.

Stakeholder feedback — from students, alumni, parents, and employers — is collected, analysed, and used to continuously refine curriculum, pedagogy, and support services, ensuring the college remains responsive to evolving industry and societal needs.

Policies

HN College of Management maintains a robust framework of institutional policies that ensure a safe, inclusive, compliant, and academically excellent environment for students, faculty, and staff.

Academic Policies

  • Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory for all theory and practical courses as per university norms.
  • Internal Assessment marks are awarded based on written tests, assignments, presentations, and participation.
  • Academic integrity is strictly upheld — plagiarism and malpractice result in disciplinary action as per the university examination rules.
  • Students with genuine medical or family emergencies may apply for attendance condonation as per the prescribed procedure.

Anti-Ragging Policy

  • HN College of Management has a zero-tolerance policy towards ragging in all its forms on and off campus.
  • A dedicated Anti-Ragging Committee actively monitors the campus and addresses all complaints with strict confidentiality and urgency.
  • Students found guilty of ragging face immediate suspension and legal action as per UGC Anti-Ragging Regulations.

Grievance Redressal Policy

  • A Grievance Redressal Committee is in place to address academic, administrative, and interpersonal complaints from students and staff.
  • All grievances are treated with strict confidentiality and resolved within a defined timeframe.
  • Students may submit grievances through the college's online grievance portal or directly to the committee in writing.

Gender Sensitisation Policy

  • The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) handles cases of sexual harassment as per the POSH Act, 2013.
  • Regular gender sensitisation workshops and awareness programmes are conducted for students and staff.
