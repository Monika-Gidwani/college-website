Policies

HN College of Management maintains a robust framework of institutional policies that ensure a safe, inclusive, compliant, and academically excellent environment for students, faculty, and staff.

Academic Policies

  • Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory for all theory and practical courses as per university norms.
  • Internal Assessment marks are awarded based on written tests, assignments, presentations, and participation.
  • Academic integrity is strictly upheld — plagiarism and malpractice result in disciplinary action as per the university examination rules.
  • Students with genuine medical or family emergencies may apply for attendance condonation as per the prescribed procedure.

Anti-Ragging Policy

  • HN College of Management has a zero-tolerance policy towards ragging in all its forms on and off campus.
  • A dedicated Anti-Ragging Committee actively monitors the campus and addresses all complaints with strict confidentiality and urgency.
  • Students found guilty of ragging face immediate suspension and legal action as per UGC Anti-Ragging Regulations.

Grievance Redressal Policy

  • A Grievance Redressal Committee is in place to address academic, administrative, and interpersonal complaints from students and staff.
  • All grievances are treated with strict confidentiality and resolved within a defined timeframe.
  • Students may submit grievances through the college's online grievance portal or directly to the committee in writing.

Gender Sensitisation Policy

  • The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) handles cases of sexual harassment as per the POSH Act, 2013.
  • Regular gender sensitisation workshops and awareness programmes are conducted for students and staff.
