Governing Body

The Governing Body of Hirachand Nemchand College of Management provides strategic direction, ensures institutional accountability, and upholds the highest standards of educational governance.

The Governing Body comprises distinguished members from academia, industry, and civil society who provide strategic oversight to the institutional functioning. It meets periodically to review academic programmes, financial management, infrastructure development, and policy implementation.
