Gymnasium & Sports

In order to facilitate all-round development and to nurture team spirit among the students, the college encourages active participation in sports.

The institute provides facilities for outdoor sports like Cricket, Volleyball, Football, Basketball, etc. and for indoor sports like Chess, Carrom, and Table Tennis. Management Premier League (MPL) is arranged within management wing for the management trophy in Cricket.
