CO, PO, PSO & PEO

Programme Educational Objectives (PEO)

  • PEO 1 – Prepare graduates for successful careers in business, industry, government, and non-profit organisations through rigorous management education.
  • PEO 2 – Develop graduates capable of analytical thinking, strategic leadership, and ethical decision-making in complex business environments.
  • PEO 3 – Foster entrepreneurial mindset and innovation to create value for organisations and communities.
  • PEO 4 – Instil a commitment to lifelong learning, professional growth, and socially responsible practice.

Programme Outcomes (PO)

  • PO 1 – Apply management theories, frameworks, and analytical tools to solve real-world business problems.
  • PO 2 – Demonstrate leadership, teamwork, communication, and negotiation skills essential in professional environments.
  • PO 3 – Exhibit ethical behaviour, corporate social responsibility, and integrity in all professional decisions.
  • PO 4 – Use quantitative methods, data analysis, and technology to support evidence-based decision-making.
  • PO 5 – Adapt to dynamic global business environments with cultural sensitivity and cross-functional knowledge.

Programme Specific Outcomes (PSO)

  • PSO 1 – Demonstrate depth in chosen specialisation (Marketing / Finance / HR / Operations / Entrepreneurship).
  • PSO 2 – Design, execute, and present a business research project using appropriate research methodology.
  • PSO 3 – Integrate cross-domain knowledge to develop comprehensive business strategies for real organisations.

Parents Meet & Orientation Program

HN College of Management conducts structured orientation and parents' meet programmes to foster a transparent, communicative relationship between the institution, students, and their families.

🎓
Induction & Orientation Programme

Conducted at the start of each academic year for newly admitted MBA students. Covers introduction to the college, department, faculty, curriculum structure, rules & regulations, library resources, and placement cell. Sessions by HOD, Principal, senior students, and industry guests. Duration: 3 Days.

👨‍👩‍👧
Parents' Meet — Semester I & III

Held at the beginning of each academic session, parents are invited to meet faculty, understand the academic calendar, discuss attendance norms, internal assessment schemes, and address queries regarding their ward's progress and college expectations.

📋
Mid-Semester Progress Review

Parents are invited to midsemester meetings where faculty share academic progress, attendance status, internal marks, and areas needing improvement. Individual counselling sessions are also available for students facing academic or personal challenges.

🏢
Pre-Placement Orientation

Before placement season, a dedicated orientation is held for students and parents, covering the placement process, companies visiting campus, expected salary ranges, and student preparation workshops by the Training & Placement Cell.
