BBA Course

The Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) programme provides a strong foundation in business, management, and entrepreneurship — grooming students for careers in business, banking, finance, marketing, and human resources, or for advanced studies such as MBA.

Duration
3 Years (6 Semesters)
Intake
60 Seats
Eligibility
10+2 (Any Stream)
Affiliation
P.A.H. Solapur University

Programme Highlights

  • Broad-based business curriculum covering management principles, accounting, economics, marketing, HR, and entrepreneurship.
  • Practical exposure through case studies, business simulations, role plays, and industry visits.
  • Industry internship in the final year to gain hands-on corporate experience.
  • Skill-development sessions in communication, leadership, presentation, and business etiquette.
  • Direct pathway to MBA — BBA graduates are preferred and prepared for top MBA entrance examinations.
  • Career pathways: Business Executive, Sales Manager, HR Executive, Banking, Retail Management, Entrepreneurship.
