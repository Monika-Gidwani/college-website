About Department

The Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) Department at Hirachand Nemchand College of Commerce develops future managers and entrepreneurs through a balanced blend of management concepts, practical learning, communication skills, and ethical values.

Duration
3 Years (6 Semesters)
Intake
60 Seats
Eligibility
10+2 (Any Stream)
Affiliation
P.A.H. Solapur University

Overview

The BBA programme provides a strong foundation in business management and prepares students for dynamic careers in marketing, finance, human resources, banking, retail, entrepreneurship, and allied sectors.

The curriculum combines classroom learning with case studies, presentations, role plays, business simulations, industry interaction, projects, and internship exposure. It helps students develop professional competence and decision-making ability.

Mission & Focus

Mission
  • Provide quality management education grounded in practical application and ethical values.
  • Develop analytical, leadership, communication, and entrepreneurial skills.
  • Create industry-ready graduates through projects, internships, seminars, and experiential learning.
What Students Learn

BBA students learn principles of management, accounting, economics, marketing, human resources, finance, business law, entrepreneurship, and strategic management.

Programme Focus

  • Management fundamentals and business decision-making
  • Accounting, finance, marketing, and human resource practices
  • Business communication and professional presentation
  • Entrepreneurship and innovation
  • Industry exposure, projects, and internship experience

PO, PSO & PEO

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

  • PEO 1: Build strong foundational knowledge of business and management concepts.
  • PEO 2: Apply managerial, analytical, and entrepreneurial skills in professional roles.
  • PEO 3: Demonstrate ethical conduct, effective communication, leadership, and lifelong learning.

Programme Outcomes (POs)

  • PO1: Understand core concepts of management, accounting, economics, finance, marketing, and human resources.
  • PO2: Analyze business problems and identify appropriate managerial solutions.
  • PO3: Use communication, presentation, and negotiation skills effectively.
  • PO4: Apply ethical, legal, and socially responsible principles in business decisions.
  • PO5: Work effectively in teams and demonstrate leadership qualities.
  • PO6: Use digital and analytical tools for business operations and decision-making.
  • PO7: Engage in lifelong learning and adapt to changing business environments.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

  • PSO1: Apply functional knowledge to solve real-world business problems.
  • PSO2: Demonstrate readiness for management, entrepreneurship, banking, or higher studies.
  • PSO3: Develop comprehensive understanding of organizational processes and industry practices.
