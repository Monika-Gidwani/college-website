Result

Semester examination results are declared by P.A.H. Solapur University. Students can check their results on the university portal or the college notice board after official declaration.

How to Check Your Result

  1. Visit the P.A.H. Solapur University Results Portal at www.sus.ac.in
  2. Click on "Student Results" or "Exam Results" link.
  3. Enter your Seat Number and select the Examination Month & Year.
  4. Click "Get Result" to view your marks and grade card.
  5. Download or print the Result for records. Original Mark Sheet is issued by the University separately.
