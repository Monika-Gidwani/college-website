Library — Books & Periodicals

The Library in HNCC, Department of Management Studies, is treated as a critical organ of the institute. Utmost importance is given to the creation of appropriate ambience for the users of the library. Accordingly our endeavor has been to identify the changing and growing needs of the readers and cater to their needs.

Objectives

  • Endeavor to create a learning environment, which enables the students to develop the habits of reading and research.
  • Adopt a data oriented factual approach that leads to continual improvement in the reading habits of all students, teachers, researchers at large and gain knowledge.
  • Develop the library into a store house of knowledge by preserving, adding, and updating books, periodicals, E-journal, Audio Visual Material etc.

Ours is a computerized library wherein all activities are being conducted by using the LIBMAN software. Database is available on OPAC (Online Public Access Catalogue) for searching. We have an open access system for our students which enable them to get an opportunity to browse through books on various subjects and select the reading material of their interest.

Our library has an excellent collection of the best books on various subjects written by famous writers, reference books, periodicals, journals, encyclopedias, yearbooks, general reading material, books for recreation, handbooks, various useful dictionaries, CD's, Audio visuals as well as DVDs and so on.

Journals

  • International Journals: To cater to needs of students and teaching faculties, 24 internationally renowned journals are being subscribed in major specializations such as Finance, Marketing, HR and General Management.
  • National Journals: To make our readers familiar with national Economic Environment, we are also subscribing to as many as 57 National Journals.
  • E-Journals: e-journals namely J-Gate, DELNET, INFLIBNET are purchased.

Library — Digital Resources

  • OPAC (Online Public Access Catalogue): Database available for searching library holdings using the LIBMAN software.
  • J-Gate: E-journal gateway providing seamless access to millions of journal articles across disciplines.
  • DELNET: Library resource sharing network for inter-library book loans and bibliographic databases.
  • INFLIBNET (N-LIST): Access to 6,000+ e-journals and 3,00,000+ e-books for students and faculty.
  • CD-ROM Service: 40 CD's and 100 Digital Books available for reference.
  • Reprography: Photocopy and scanning facility available in the library at nominal charges.
  • Bar-coding System: Modern barcode reader-based circulation system for fast book issue and return.

Our TV provides the students with latest information about the market position, information on shares and statistical data and many current affairs and transactions.

Library — Rules & Regulations

  • Library membership is compulsory for all registered students. ID card must be produced at entry and during book issue.
  • Students may borrow a maximum of 3 books at a time for a period of 14 days.
  • Overdue books attract a fine of ₹2 per book per day after the due date.
  • Talking, using mobile phones, and eating/drinking are strictly prohibited inside the library.
  • Torn, damaged, or lost books must be replaced or fined at double the cost of the book as per library rules.
  • Reference books, journals, and newspapers are for in-library use only and cannot be issued for home use.
  • Silence must be maintained in the library at all times.
  • Library membership is non-transferable.
