Examination

HN College of Management, being an Autonomous College, conducts its own university-level semester examinations as per the examination schedule prescribed by P.A.H. Solapur University.

Examination Pattern

Component Marks Frequency
Internal Assessment (Test I + Test II + Assignments)30Each Semester
University / End-Semester Examination70Each Semester
Total100

Examination Rules

  • Students must carry their College ID Card and Hall Ticket to every examination.
  • Minimum 75% attendance is required to be eligible to appear in university examinations.
  • Use of unfair means during examination is a serious offence and may lead to cancellation of the examination paper and disciplinary action.
  • Students must report to the examination hall 15 minutes before the scheduled start time.
  • Mobile phones and electronic devices are strictly prohibited in the examination hall.
  • Re-assessment / Photocopy of Answer Books can be applied for as per university norms within the prescribed time limit.
  • Atkt / Supplementary examinations are conducted as per university schedule.
