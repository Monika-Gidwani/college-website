About Internal Committee

The Internal Committee (IC) is constituted as per the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. The committee addresses complaints related to sexual harassment and ensures a safe work environment for all staff and students.
